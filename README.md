# FinalProjectAstro100
We present the results of observational experiments performed using the FAST Telescope
at Mount Hopkins Observatory in Arizona. The galaxies observed are NGC 4157, UGC 4972,
and M 104. These were selected due to their face-on position during the night of observations
and their air mass- the amount of air in our line of sight from Earth towards the celestial body-
being under 2.0. From our observations we were able to discover how to calibrate science
images based on their wavelength and redshift, and compute their respective properties while
still analyzing the flaws associated with our observational method. In this paper, we present
NGC 4157, UGC 4972, and M 104’s velocity rotations, redshift, and total mass.
